Which ring are you on?

Five picks. Shows how finely you name what you feel.

Five moments. For each, the word you would most genuinely feel — not the one that reads best. In every set, all three words come from the same family on the wheel above: the center word, then one ring out, then one ring further. All three are honest answers — they differ only in resolution.

Where’s your confidence pointed?

Every statement is confident. Only the direction is in question.
