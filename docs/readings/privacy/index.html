Privacy

What is collected when you buy a Human Design reading, what is kept, for how long, and who else ever sees it.

Last updated October 23, 2026.

The birth details you type — date, time, place and time zone — are used to work out your chart, and they are kept with your chart so your page and PDF can show when and where you were born. They are kept for the same year as your chart, and they are never sent to Google, who writes the interpretation. The summary keeps them too, so going a step further never means typing them again.The form also asks whose chart it will be, if you’d like to give a name. It becomes the title of your page and your PDF (“Alex’s Human Design”), and it is kept with your chart in the same way as your birth details: in the same place, for the same year, and deleted with them. Google never sees it.If your chart was made without a birth time, you’re welcome to add it later, once and free. Your earlier details, and the chart (and reading) made from them, stay with your chart for its year, seen only by Jeremy.

The birth details changed on October 16, 2026, the name for the chart on October 19, 2026, the summary keeping them too on October 20, 2026, and adding a birth time later on October 23, 2026, so they come first. Everything below is the ordinary detail.

Why the link is short and the reading is not

They are two different clocks on purpose. The link is a key: anyone holding it can open your reading, so it stops working after six days in case it is forwarded, left in an old inbox, or read over your shoulder. The reading is the thing you bought, so it keeps for the year regardless.

An expired link is not a dead end and you do not have to write in for it. Open it and the page offers you a button that sends a fresh one — to the address on your purchase and nowhere else, as many times as you need, for the whole year. That is the only address it will ever go to, which is what stops the button from being a way for somebody else to have your reading mailed to them.

One link works differently. When a chart is made without a birth time, its PDF carries a link for adding the time later, and that link does not stop after six days, because a PDF is kept for years. It can do one thing only: add a birth time to that one chart, once. The page it opens shows nothing that is not already on the PDF. Once the time is added, it opens the chart made with it, and after that it only says the time has been added. It works for as long as the chart is kept.

How it is kept safe

Plainly, and without overstating it:

  • Everything travels encrypted. Every connection to this site, and every connection from it to Stripe, Google, Resend and the chart calculator, is over HTTPS.
  • Everything sits encrypted. Your reading is stored by Netlify, which encrypts it at rest on their disks.
  • Your reading is not reachable by guessing. It is filed under a name derived from your payment with a secret key, and the link is signed. There is no page that lists readings and no address you can count up to.
  • Your card is never here. It goes from you to Stripe. This site never receives it and could not store it if it wanted to.
  • Your birth details are kept only beside your chart — the date, the time, the place you picked and its time zone. Your PDF prints the date, the time and the place; the time zone is kept because your chart is worked out from it. No map coordinates are kept, and they are never filed anywhere on their own. The name you give the chart, if you give one, is kept beside your reading in the same way.

Who else sees any of it

A handful of companies do specific jobs, and each one gets only what that job needs.

  • Stripe takes the payment and holds your card details. They receive your name and email.
  • Resend delivers the emails. They receive your email address and what the message says.
  • Google writes the interpretation on the reading tier, using their Gemini model. They receive the values of your chart and nothing else — no name (not yours, and not the one you gave the chart), no email, no birth date, no birth time, no place. What is sent looks like “Projector, 1/3, Self-Projected, channels 11-56 and 1-8”, attached to nobody.
  • Netlify hosts the site and stores your reading.
  • Fly.io runs the chart calculator. Your birth date, time and time zone pass through it to be computed — never your name or the place — and are not stored there.

Nothing is sold, rented, or handed to advertisers. There is no advertising here at all.

Being emailed

You are emailed about the thing you bought — that it is ready, and again if you ask for the link. That is delivery, and it is not permission to market to you. You will not be added to a mailing list because you bought a reading. If that ever changes you will be asked first, separately, and in plain words.

What you can ask for

  • A copy of everything held about you.
  • Deletion. Your reading and your details can be removed before the year is up, and it will be done rather than debated. Ask, and it happens.
  • A correction to your name, email or birth details, or to the name on your chart.
  • Your reading again — the link can be re-sent to the address you bought with, as often as you need, for the whole year. If you have lost the email, “Email me my charts” on the Human Design readings page sends a fresh link to each chart bought with your address.

One email to hd-readings@thechampagnemethod.co does any of these. There is no form and no process; it is one person reading it.

Depending on where you live you may have these rights in law as well — under the GDPR in the UK and Europe, or state privacy laws in the US. They are offered here to everybody regardless.

If something goes wrong

The site watches itself and reports failures to Jeremy automatically. If a problem ever exposed somebody’s information, the people affected would be told what happened and what was done about it — not left to find out.

Children

This is not intended for anyone under 16, and readings are not knowingly sold to children. If a purchase was made by one, write in and it will be refunded and erased. Purchases are otherwise final, with the two exceptions named before you pay: if something did not arrive, or did not work, that is Jeremy’s to put right; and if the birth details went in wrong, write in and he will do what he can.

When this page changes

The rule, so it is not left to memory: this page is updated in the same piece of work that changes how information is handled — not afterward, and not when somebody notices. If a change adds something collected, adds a company that sees it, changes how long anything is kept, or turns on analytics, the page changes with it and the date at the top moves.

A change that affects what is collected or who sees it will be said plainly here rather than slipped in. Anyone whose reading is still within its year at the time will be told by email as well.
