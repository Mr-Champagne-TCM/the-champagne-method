# How to register "The Champagne Method" — USPTO

Research notes, 2026-08-24. Not legal advice; a trademark attorney should sanity-check
class selection and the specimen before filing.

## The finding that makes this worth doing
USPTO search (exact phrase, all classes): every existing "champagne method" record is a
**Class 033 wine mark** (mostly dead). **No one — including Kristie Christensen — has
filed for the name in any coaching, education, or business-services class.** First
qualifying filer gets the presumption of nationwide rights.

## Classes to consider

| Class | Covers | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **041** | Education; providing of training; coaching services; workshops | **Core.** Covers life coaching AND the future coach-training expansion — one class covers both |
| 035 | Business consulting, personal branding services | Only if TCM ever sells business/brand consulting (this is *her* lane — filing here invites the conflict) |
| 044 | Counseling/health-adjacent services | Usually overkill for coaching; attorney call |

**Recommendation shape:** file **Class 041 only** to start. It covers "coaching in the
field of personal development" and "training of coaches" — the stated expansion path —
for one class fee, and stays out of her Class-035 territory.

## Filing basis — the expansion question answered
Two bases, and they can be combined in one application:
- **§1(a) use in commerce** — for what's already being sold: personal coaching under the
  mark (live site, paying clients). Needs a specimen (screenshot of the site offering
  the service works) and a first-use date.
- **§1(b) intent to use** — for services not yet launched (coach training). Locks
  priority now; prove use later via a Statement of Use.
A single Class-041 application can list both current coaching services (1a) and
coach-training (1b).

## Costs (USPTO fees, per the 2025 fee structure — verify at filing)
- Base application: **$350 per class**
- Surcharges to avoid: +$200/class for free-text descriptions — **use ID Manual
  descriptions only**; +$100/class for incomplete applications
- If using §1(b): Statement of Use later **$150/class**; extensions (6-month, up to 5)
  **$125–160 each** if launch slips
- Attorney: typically $500–1,500 flat for a straightforward application — recommended
  here because of the Christensen coexistence question

So: ~$350 filing + SOU $150 + attorney ≈ **$1,000–2,000 all-in** for Class 041.

## Process
1. Collect first-use evidence (see EVIDENCE.md) and pick a defensible first-use date
2. Attorney consult: clearance opinion re: Christensen; confirm classes/basis
3. File via USPTO TEAS (uspto.gov → Trademarks → Apply)
4. Examination ~8–12 months; respond to any office action
5. Publication for opposition (30 days) — **this is where Christensen could object**,
   and where prior-use evidence matters
6. Registration (1a portions) / Notice of Allowance → SOU (1b portions)
7. Maintenance: §8 declaration years 5–6, renewal every 10 years

## Risks, honestly
- **Opposition:** she could oppose during publication. Her observable use starts
  Dec 2025–Apr 2026; Jeremy's name-use evidence starts 2022. Different service classes
  also help. Attorney assesses the real odds.
- **Descriptiveness pushback:** unlikely — "champagne method" is arguably suggestive
  for coaching (it's literally descriptive only for sparkling wine), and it's his
  surname, which has its own doctrine (surname marks need acquired distinctiveness —
  another reason for the attorney conversation).
- **Doing nothing** also has a cost: if she (or anyone) files first in Class 041,
  Jeremy is the one proving prior use from the defensive side.
